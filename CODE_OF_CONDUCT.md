# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our standards

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, trolling, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainer through https://github.com/musichen.
